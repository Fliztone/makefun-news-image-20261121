# news-image-20261121 - World news of 21 November 2026 in one image

Entry for the make.fun bounty "Sum up 21 November's world news in one image" ($100).

30 verified stories from Saturday 21 November 2026 across 6 regions: the Gulf on the brink (IRGC strikes on US bases, Trump threats), the Gaza aid convoy strike, the US cyber chaos (FBI database breach, cyber chief fired), the EU big-tech tax proposal, the Nasdaq AI sell-off, Denmark expelling Russia's attache, the M6.8 Banda Sea quake, plus 24 more headlines spanning US politics, chips, Asia, Europe, sport and media.

Sources: AP, Anadolu, ToI, Al Jazeera, DW, NPR, NYT, CBS, CNBC, Guardian, Japan Times, ET, APA, MEM, Reuters, GFZ, Athletic, all reporting on 21 November 2026.

## Run

    node main.ts

Writes entry.png (1024x1024, 157KB) into $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR, default /output. Nothing is fetched at run time: the poster is committed, so two runs produce the same bytes.
